Credits

      

        
DEAD AIR is a short horror shooter presented as recovered body-worn camera footage. Everything in it runs live in your browser: the city block, the rain, the people, the sound.

        
WHAT IS PROCEDURAL

        
Every body is sculpted at load time from signed-distance anatomy, meshed with surface nets and skinned automatically. Deaths and corpses are simulated with a verlet ragdoll. All sounds, including gunshots, rain and zombie voices, are synthesised with Web Audio. The camera look (fisheye lens, chromatic aberration, auto exposure, sensor noise, compression and lens water) is a custom post-processing chain.

        
THIRD-PARTY ASSETS

        
Surface textures, props and HDR environments: Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0. Rendering: three.js (MIT). Type: Big Shoulders Display and IBM Plex (SIL Open Font License).

        
FICTION

        
All names, units, places and devices are fictional.
